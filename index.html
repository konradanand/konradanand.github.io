Probability · Combinatorics · Algorithms

Counting, sampling, and randomization

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Oxford, hosted by Leslie Goldberg.

My research sits at the intersection of probability, combinatorics, and algorithms, with a particular focus on efficient methods for approximate counting and sampling.

Before Oxford, I was a postdoc at the University of Edinburgh from 2024 to 2026, hosted by Heng Guo. I completed my PhD in Mathematics at Queen Mary University of London with Mark Jerrum, and my MSc in Computer Science and BSc Honours in Mathematics at McGill University; my MSc was supervised by Luc Devroye.
